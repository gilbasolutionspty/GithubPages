01

Vertmax and Vertmax Duo turf pigments

Vertmax is a concentrated turf pigment made in Australia, with a high pigment load and Quick Dry Technology. It gives warm- or cool-season grass an even, natural green, which makes it useful for keeping dormant couch presentable through the cooler months. Once it has dried, which takes about an hour, it does not come back off with dew or irrigation.

Vertmax Duo uses the same pigment concentrate and adds a bio-stimulant package based on salicylic acid. Gilba recommends it for golf greens, and it was chosen for a 2026 FIFA World Cup venue, BC Stadium.

02

SeeGrow LED grow lights for shaded and worn turf

Gilba Solutions is the Australian agent for SeeGrow stadium grow lights. Each rig uses individual LED diodes tuned to a turfgrass-specific light spectrum, and the lights give off no heat, so they can sit over the playing surface without scorching it. They are used for stadium turf in shade, quick recovery after off-season events, overseeding and transition, and high-wear areas such as goalmouths.

CommBank Stadium in Parramatta was the first venue in Australia to use turfgrass-specific LED grow lights. Overseas users listed by Gilba include Chelsea FC, Arsenal FC, Juventus and Aviva Stadium.

Omne Turf Wi-Fi sensing boxes

Omne Turf boxes bring Internet of Things monitoring to greens, fairways and pitches. Each small, solar-powered unit streams readings around the clock to a dashboard you open in an ordinary web browser, with growing degree days and disease potential worked out from local conditions. There is no cabling, so a box can be moved to watch a problem area.

Soil wetting agents matched to the problem

Sandy soils and soils high in organic matter can develop a waxy coating that sheds water. Wetting agents break that barrier, but they do different jobs, so Gilba groups them by purpose:

Penetrants

Move water quickly down through hydrophobic sands and localised dry spots. Examples: HydroForce Rapid, Scrubwet.

Retainers

Hold moisture in the root zone for longer on drought-prone or coarse soils. Examples: HydroForce Extend, Aquimax.

Combination agents

A penetrant and retainer in one, for high-traffic turf and sports surfaces. Examples: HydroForce Ultra, Aquaforce.

In Gilba's own 2024 trials, HydroForce Ultra held 15% more moisture at 75 mm depth than untreated plots. Compare the wetting agents on gilbasolutions.com.

Weeds and algae in irrigation dams
